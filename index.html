Deep Nexivra Resume Library

Resume guides built around your career situation.

Use these as decision guides, then take the advice straight into Resume Studio, Match Lab, or Tailor Studio. Keep every claim evidence-based.

🎓
Early career

Student & New Graduate

Build credibility when your professional history is still short.

ProjectsCourseworkInternships
Open guide
Prioritize

Relevant projects, placements, part-time work, volunteering, tools, coursework, and concrete outcomes.

Avoid

Generic “hard worker” statements, unsupported proficiency claims, and filling space with unrelated duties.

Best structure

Target headline → concise summary → skills/tools → education/projects → experience, ordered by what proves the target role best.

🧭
Transition

Career Change

Show why your previous experience transfers without pretending it was the same job.

Transferable skillsTarget languageEvidence
Open guide
Prioritize

Responsibilities and outcomes that map directly to the new role: coordination, analysis, operations, stakeholder work, quality, leadership, or systems.

Avoid

Changing historical job titles or inserting tools and responsibilities you never used.

Best structure

Target-facing summary → transferable capabilities → strongest aligned achievements → supporting experience and education.

🪖
Translation

Military to Civilian

Translate service scope into language a civilian recruiter can understand.

LeadershipOperationsScale
Open guide
Prioritize

Team size, equipment or budget responsibility, logistics, safety, training, planning, readiness, and measurable mission outcomes.

Avoid

Unexplained acronyms and rank-specific terminology when a plain civilian equivalent communicates the function better.

Best structure

Civilian target headline → capability summary → translated achievements → service history → certifications/clearances when relevant.

🩺
Clinical & technical

Healthcare & Laboratory

Make quality, patient impact, workflow, instrumentation, and compliance easy to see.

QualityWorkflowCompliance
Open guide
Prioritize

Specimen or patient workflow, instruments/systems, SOP adherence, safety, quality checks, turnaround, volumes, issue resolution, and cross-functional coordination.

Avoid

Claiming regulated scope, licensing, procedures, or independent authority beyond what you actually performed.

Best structure

Credential/status where relevant → lab specialization → tools/processes → evidence-rich experience → education/certifications.

⚙️
Technical

Engineering & Technical

Connect technical decisions to reliability, cost, throughput, quality, or delivery.

SystemsProjectsMetrics
Open guide
Prioritize

Tools, methods, systems, design or process decisions, project scope, constraints, quantified improvements, and technical ownership.

Avoid

Long tool lists with no evidence showing where or how you used them.

Best structure

Technical summary → skills/tools → selected projects/achievements → experience → education and credentials.

📈
Senior leadership

Executive & Senior Leadership

Shift the resume from task history to enterprise scope, ownership, and outcomes.

StrategyScaleLeadership
Open guide
Prioritize

Business scope, P&L or budget where supported, team size, transformation, growth, risk, operating model, stakeholder influence, and strategic outcomes.

Avoid

Dense duty lists and vague leadership adjectives that do not show scale or impact.

Best structure

Executive profile → selected leadership outcomes → core expertise → reverse-chronological experience with strategic bullets.

🧩
Business execution

Project & Operations

Show how you coordinate work, improve processes, and deliver outcomes across teams.

DeliveryProcessStakeholders
Open guide
Prioritize

Projects, timelines, workflow improvements, reporting, capacity, risk/issues, stakeholder coordination, tools, and measurable delivery results.

Avoid

Using “managed” when you only assisted; accurately distinguish ownership, coordination, and support.

Best structure

Targeted summary → project/operations skills → relevant achievements → experience → certifications and tools.

ATS-Safe Resume

Keep the document readable for both parsing systems and human reviewers.

Simple structureKeywordsTruth-first
Open guide
Prioritize

Standard headings, readable chronology, relevant terminology from the job posting, plain text structure, and evidence-backed bullets.

Avoid

Keyword stuffing, decorative graphics as information, complex tables, unsupported skills, and hiding important content in headers/footers.

Deep Nexivra rule

A missing requirement should stay a visible gap unless your Career Graph contains real evidence that supports it.
