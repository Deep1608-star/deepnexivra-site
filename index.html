THE THESIS

Most business problems become operations problems before they become expensive problems.

Growth gets messy when decisions, people, tools, handoffs, priorities, and information stop moving together.

That is where I operate: between strategy and day-to-day execution. I turn the way work actually happens into a system people can understand, run, measure, and improve.

Unclear ownership · reactive work · manual handoffs · invisible bottlenecks

AFTER Flow
